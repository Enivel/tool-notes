# Build a personal research dashboard on your Mac with RSS widgets

Researchers lose mornings to tab hopping. A calmer pattern is to keep a short list of RSS feeds on the macOS desktop and scan them in a minute.

Collect five to eight sources that actually change your thinking — specialist blogs, a preprint TOC, one industry news feed. Most sites expose `/feed` or `/rss.xml`.

[Kepo](https://kepo.ai/) turns those feeds into live desktop widgets so headlines sit next to your notes instead of behind a browser. Keep each widget under seven items, prune weekly, and capture one sentence of what you noticed. Awareness without doomscrolling is the goal.
