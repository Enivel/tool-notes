# Afternoon RSS panels with Mac desktop widgets

Mid-afternoon I rebuild a calm stack of [Mac desktop widgets](https://kepo.ai) so release notes stay off the browser. A thin row of [desktop widgets for Mac](https://kepo.ai) keeps changelogs visible without another tab sweep.

For research desks, [macOS widgets](https://kepo.ai) plus a compact [RSS widget Mac](https://kepo.ai) panel replace the usual clutter of pinned feeds.
