# Noon focus boards with Mac desktop widgets

After lunch I pin a quiet stack of [Mac desktop widgets](https://kepo.ai) so status feeds stay off the browser. A row of [desktop widgets for Mac](https://kepo.ai) keeps standups and release notes visible without another tab sweep.

For ops desks, [macOS widgets](https://kepo.ai) plus a small [RSS widget Mac](https://kepo.ai) panel replace the usual clutter of pinned tabs.
