# A what-changed-overnight widget pack for morning review

Start the day with a pull-based overnight diff, not with whoever shouted in Slack. On a Mac, assemble three small widgets: Ops (status pages + dependency releases), World (two industry RSS feeds), Personal (one feed tied to your current project).

[Kepo](https://kepo.ai/) can render those RSS/Atom sources as desktop widgets. Spend five minutes: note surprises, triage ignore/schedule/act, then switch Spaces into deep work — email after the pack, not before. Remove any feed that never produces action. Intention beats inbox.
