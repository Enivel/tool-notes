# Keeping market and news feeds on a Mac desktop with widgets

Most of us check the same five things dozens of times a day: a stock or crypto price, a couple of news sources, a GitHub repo, the weather, maybe a Hacker News thread. Each check means opening a tab, waiting, and getting distracted by everything else on the page.

A calmer setup is to pin those feeds directly on the desktop. [Kepo](https://kepo.ai/) is a macOS app that turns websites, RSS feeds and data sources into live desktop widgets, so the information sits next to your work instead of behind a browser tab.

## A simple layout that works

1. **One market widget** with the three to five tickers you actually trade or watch. More than that becomes noise.
2. **One news widget** fed by RSS from two or three sources you trust.
3. **One "work" widget**: a changelog, a status page or a repo's releases.
4. Leave the rest of the screen empty. The point is fewer things, not more.

## Habits that keep it useful

- Review the widgets once a week and delete anything you have not glanced at.
- Prefer feeds that update slowly (daily summaries) over real-time tickers if you get anxious watching prices.
- Put widgets on a secondary display or a corner of the main screen, not in the centre.

The result is a desktop that answers "what changed?" in a single glance, without opening a browser at all.
