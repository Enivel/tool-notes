# Afternoon check of snippets in an online HTML viewer

Before I paste a generated block into Notion or email, I drop it into an [online HTML viewer](https://viewhtml.app). A quick [HTML preview](https://viewhtml.app) catches broken tags and missing styles.

Share links from a [live HTML preview](https://viewhtml.app) beat screenshots when reviewing with clients, and an [online HTML preview](https://viewhtml.app) keeps the demo off my laptop.
